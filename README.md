# AICTE_internship_potatoLeaf_project
Potato crops are highly susceptible to diseases that can significantly impact yield and quality. Traditional disease detection methods rely on manual inspection, which is often time-consuming, subjective, and prone to errors. This study aims to develop an AI-based system for detecting and classifying potato leaf diseases efficiently. By leveraging Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), the proposed system automates disease identification, improving accuracy and reducing human dependency. A dataset of healthy and diseased potato leaf images is collected and preprocessed for training. The CNN model is trained to recognize common diseases such as late blight, early blight, and bacterial wilt. Image processing techniques are used for feature extraction and classification, ensuring precise disease identification. Experimental results demonstrate that CNN-based models achieve high accuracy compared to traditional machine learning approaches. The system enables faster and more reliable disease detection and can be integrated into mobile applications, drones, or IoT-based agricultural monitoring systems for real-time diagnosis. The findings highlight the potential of deep learning in precision agriculture, offering a cost-effective, scalable, and efficient solution for farmers. By enabling early detection, this approach minimizes crop losses and enhances agricultural productivity. Future work can focus on expanding the dataset, optimizing model performance, and integrating IoT sensors for continuous real-time monitoring and disease prevention.
